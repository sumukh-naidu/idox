# Regional Breakdown
The table below breaks down active users by region for the most recent month. North America and
Europe together account for the majority of the user base.
| Region | Active Users | % of Total | MoM Change |
| North America | 84,200 | 40% | +6% |
| Europe | 63,500 | 30% | +4% |
| Asia Pacific | 42,000 | 20% | +9% |
| Rest of World | 21,000 | 10% | +2% |
Asia Pacific showed the fastest month-over-month growth at 9%, suggesting continued investment in
that region is likely to pay off over the next two quarters.
